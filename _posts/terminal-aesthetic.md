The terminal aesthetic isn't about nostalgia. It's about constraint. Monospace fonts force a grid. The grid forces honest hierarchy. Honest hierarchy means you can't hide weak content behind a fancy layout.

## What I kept

- A monospace font for everything
- Two colors: foreground and muted
- Square-bracketed nav links that look like file paths

## What I avoided

The mistake most "terminal" sites make is leaning into ASCII art and command-prompt gimmicks. That's costume, not design. Skip it. The aesthetic works because the typography is honest, not because it pretends to be a shell.
